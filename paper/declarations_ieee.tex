\section*{Data Availability}

The data are public: CICIoT2023 is distributed by the Canadian Institute for
Cybersecurity~\cite{neto2023ciciot}; the supplement's ResNet-20 rounds use CIFAR-10.
The pipeline, the decision mask, the counter-falsifying attacker, the per-round logs,
the registration documents with their hash manifests and all result files are at
\url{\repourl}; every table and figure is regenerated from them by
\texttt{paper/make\_numbers.py} and \texttt{paper/make\_figs.py}.
